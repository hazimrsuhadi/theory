Terms & Working Policies

  • Short-Notice Booking: Engagements requested with under 48 hours' notice incur a 50% surcharge.
  • Travel & Location: In-person calls within DKI Jakarta are billed round-trip at standard ride-hailing rates.
  • Minimum Concert Call: Events requiring venue call or standby time are billed at the minimum call rate (Rp 1.280.000) to cover event lockout.
  • Choir Competitions & Festivals: Billed on a Half-Day (up to 4 hrs) or Full-Day (up to 8 hrs) block basis. Out-of-town or multi-day festival engagements are billed at a daily rate of Rp 3.000.000,00 – Rp 3.500.000,00, with travel, lodging, and per diem covered by the hiring ensemble.
  • Extended Packages: Projects requiring more than 5 rehearsal hours are eligible for customized package pricing.
  • Multi-Soloist Showcases: Billed at Rp 250.000 per soloist (or Rp 380.000 per duet/ensemble piece). Engagements with fewer than 5 items are subject to the standard Minimum Concert Call floor of Rp 1.280.000, which is automatically waived when 5 or more items are booked.
