Terms & Working Policies

  • Short-Notice Booking: Engagements requested with under 48 hours' notice incur a 50% surcharge.
  • Travel & Location: In-person calls within DKI Jakarta are billed round-trip at standard ride-hailing rates.
  • Minimum Concert Call: Events requiring venue call or standby time (e.g. accompanying 1–3 pieces in a full choir concert) are billed at the minimum call rate (Rp 1.280.000) to cover event lockout.
  • Choir Competitions & Festivals: Billed on a Half-Day (up to 4 hrs) or Full-Day (up to 8 hrs) block basis. Out-of-town or multi-day festival engagements are billed at a daily rate of Rp 3.000.000,00 – Rp 3.500.000,00, with travel, private lodging, and per diem covered by the hiring ensemble.
  • Extended Rehearsal Packages: Projects requiring more than 5 rehearsal hours are eligible for customized package pricing.
  • Multi-Soloist Showcases: Concerts featuring 3+ soloists are billed per performer or via custom full-day production rates.
